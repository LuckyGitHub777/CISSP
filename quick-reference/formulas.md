# Formulas

## Risk and Loss

```text
SLE = Asset Value × Exposure Factor
ALE = SLE × ARO
Residual Risk = Risk remaining after controls
```

## Continuity

```text
RTO = Recovery Time Objective
RPO = Recovery Point Objective
MTD = Maximum Tolerable Downtime
```

## Rule

Formulas support judgment. They do not replace risk ownership, legal review, business impact analysis, or executive decision-making.
