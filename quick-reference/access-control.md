# Access Control

## Core Concepts

- Identification
- Authentication
- Authorization
- Accountability
- Least privilege
- Separation of duties
- Privileged access management
- Access reviews
- Provisioning
- Deprovisioning
- Service accounts
- Non-human identities

## Leadership Rules

- No identity, no access.
- No owner, no privilege.
- No review, no trust.
- Remove access when business purpose ends.
